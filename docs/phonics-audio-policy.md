# Phonics audio policy

Every consolidated pronunciation section uses only its corresponding category-specific synthesized recordings. A sound category has one playback control; elision and liaison example categories retain their own exact-example recordings. The old classroom/human pronunciation players are not rendered in any unit.

`originalLessonAudio` centrally suppresses phonics titles. The consolidated pronunciation renderer never prepends human audio. Original audio files remain archived; non-phonics verb, dialogue and reading recordings retain their existing behavior.

Validation covers all seven visible units with pronunciation content (source lesson IDs 02, 03, 05, 06, 07, 08 and 13): no human player appears in the pronunciation panel, and every sound group has exactly one category playback control. The two existing weather dialogue/reading recordings remain available.
