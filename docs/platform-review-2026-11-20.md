Desktop and 390px mobile screens were captured and inspected using a fresh headless browser and dummy account because the in-app browser RPC was unavailable. No real credentials were used.

## Random review

Listening and reading both expose Random review. It samples up to ten questions uniformly without replacement from the current account's completed groups in that skill. Empty pools disable the button. Small pools use all available questions. Existing and imported groups share the renderer; a source tag and original set/question remain visible. Review responses are temporary and do not alter original answers or completion. Starting again reshuffles. Leaving returns to normal practice. Questions are loaded as needed, rather than downloading every completed set's audio.
